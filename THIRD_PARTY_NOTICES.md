# Third-party notices

## Station pictograms (`station-icons.svg`)

The per-station icons are traced from the icon font in
[yorkfx/icons-metro-df](https://github.com/yorkfx/icons-metro-df) by Miguel
Mendez, itself based on the pictogram system designed for the Sistema de
Transporte Colectivo (Mexico City Metro) under Pedro Ramírez Vázquez, with
iconography by Lance Wyman.

- The CSS/font packaging in `icons-metro-df` is MIT licensed.
- The underlying station pictogram designs are registered with Mexico's
  Instituto Nacional del Derecho de Autor; per that repository's README,
  their use, reproduction, modification, or commercialization is restricted
  without written permission from the Sistema de Transporte Colectivo.

These icons are used here for non-commercial, fan-made purposes to identify
real Mexico City Metro stations by name, matching their original purpose on
the transit system itself.

The exceptions are `azcapotzalco` (UAM Azcapotzalco) and `zapotitlan`
(Zapotitlán), since the `icons-metro-df` font has no icon for either
station: both are traced from station images in
[ronihdzz/metro-maps-cdmx](https://github.com/ronihdzz/metro-maps-cdmx)
(GPL-3.0), whose README states its station images are themselves sourced
from the official Mexico City Metro website - so the same STC/Lance Wyman
attribution and rights note above applies to these icons as well.
